Welcome to UniLostFound

UniLostFound is a simple platform for managing lost and found items within the university.

Students can report lost or found items, search for items, and submit claims to recover their belongings.

What You Can Do:
Report Lost/Found Item Report details of a lost or found item.
Search Items Search and view lost or found items.
Submit Claims Submit a claim and track its status.
